## 👨‍💻 About Me

An experienced **Odoo consultant** with a deep understanding of ERP solutions and business optimization. I'm the **Executive Director at [elblasy.app](https://elblasy.app)**, where I help companies implement, customize, and scale Odoo.

- 🏭 Specialized in **Manufacturing, Inventory, POS, Accounting & Sales** workflows
- 🐳 Automating Odoo deployments with **Docker, Shell & Kubernetes**
- 🧩 Building custom modules, integrations, and reports
- 📚 Sharing guides, e.g. [Odoo 18 Manufacturing Guide](https://github.com/elblasy33/odoo-18-manufacturing-guide)
- 🌐 Portfolio: [elblasy.app/portfolio](https://elblasy.app/portfolio)
